Ritual privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Ritual is a habit tracker for Android. It works fully offline and without an account. This policy explains what happens to your information, including when you choose to sign in to back up your habits.

Without an account

Your habits, completion history, reminder times, focus sessions and settings are stored only on your phone. Ritual doesn't send them anywhere.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and is used only to back up and restore your data. When you sign in, Ritual uses:

  • Your Google account's name and email address and a sign-in identifier, to create your Ritual account and show which account is connected.
  • Your habit data: habit names and descriptions, the days you completed them, reminder settings, and your appearance and haptics settings. These are copied to your private cloud backup so you can restore them after reinstalling or on a new phone.

Ritual doesn't request access to your contacts, files, location, Google Drive or any other Google data. Focus session timers stay on your phone.

Where it's stored

Backups are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes the data on Ritual's behalf. Data is encrypted in transit. Access rules ensure each account can read and write only its own data. Google may process technical information such as IP addresses to operate and secure these services; see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

What Ritual doesn't do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Notifications and permissions

Reminders and focus timer alerts are scheduled on your phone. Ritual asks for notification permission only when you turn on a reminder or start a focus session. It uses vibration for subtle touch feedback, following your phone's touch-feedback settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your backup is kept until you delete your account. You can delete your account and everything backed up to it at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account, or by following the steps on the account deletion page. Deletion is permanent. Habits on your phone stay there until you remove them, for example by uninstalling Ritual.

Children

Ritual isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. For questions or requests, contact the developer at the email address shown on Ritual's Google Play page.
