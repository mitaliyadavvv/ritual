Delete your Ritual account

Ritual works without an account. If you signed in with Google to back up your habits, you can delete that account and everything backed up to it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Ritual and tap the Settings button at the top of any tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account, then confirm.
  3. Choose your Google account when asked. This confirms it's you.

Without the app

If you no longer have Ritual installed, email the developer at the contact address shown on Ritual's Google Play page. Send it from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Ritual account". Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days and you'll get a confirmation.

What is deleted

Your Ritual account (Google sign-in) and all data backed up to it: habits, completion history, reminder times and appearance settings. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. No backup copies are kept.

What isn't

Habits stored on your phone stay there and keep working offline until you remove them yourself, for example by uninstalling Ritual.
